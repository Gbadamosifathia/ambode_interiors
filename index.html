The story behind Ambode Interiors

Even as a child, I would walk into a room and wonder how the furniture in it was made. That curiosity never left. As I grew older, I went to technical school to learn the craft, then trained under experienced hands to master the practical side of the work.

I've been building professionally for over six years, and I recently added a National Diploma in Carpentry from Yabatech to my experience. My favourite moment is when a client shows me a picture of what they imagine and I bring it to life in wood, fabric and finish. Seeing how happy and satisfied they are when it's done is what keeps me doing this.
